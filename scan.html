Who is actually talking?

Three measured signals on a token's conversation, each ranked against every other token scanned. No sign-up, no email.

What this does and does not tell you

Each signal is a measurement, and the percentile is a rank against the other tokens scanned rather than a threshold invented here. A token in the 90th percentile is being compared with real peers, not with an idea of what normal looks like.

None of it says who is behind anything. Repeated wording across accounts is consistent with a paid campaign, an airdrop task, a quote-tweet chain, or a community handed a template, and this will not tell you which.

The larger thing it cannot see is whether any of the attention produced buyers. That needs onchain work, which takes hours per token and is part of a paid audit.
